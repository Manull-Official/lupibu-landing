About

An iOS app that learns each baby's sleep rhythm and predicts what's likely next.

01Problem

Tracking apps record everything but answer nothing. New parents end up with history they don't have time to read. Lupibu turns that history into a forecast — what's likely next — and an AI assistant that already knows the context.

02Product

An iPhone app that learns each baby's rhythm and predicts when the next sleep window will open. The AI assistant answers parenting questions with full history as context.

03How it works

What's inside the app

Sleep-rhythm prediction, an iOS widget for the next nap, voice and AI-assisted logging, an AI assistant with full history, dark mode, and a paediatrician-ready PDF.

04Who it's for

Parents tracking a baby's daily routine. Multilingual households are a core use case — voice input works in three languages from day one.

05Market

Consumer digital health and parenting tech — infant tracking, sleep guidance, AI-powered family assistants. The category is shifting from passive logs to prediction-driven products.
