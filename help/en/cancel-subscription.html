How to cancel Pro

1 min read

In short: Cancel anytime via Apple ID → Subscriptions. Pro access stays until the end of the paid period.

Cancellation is one tap, no phone calls, no dark patterns. If Pro isn't for you, we'd rather part cleanly.

How to cancel

  • Open iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions
  • Find Lupibu Pro
  • Tap "Cancel Subscription"

What happens

  • Pro features stay until the end of the already-paid period
  • Auto-renew turns off
  • All your data stays — come back to Pro anytime

Canceling during the trial

Nothing charged. Pro stays until the trial ends — 7 or 14 days, depending on which offer you accepted.

Refunds

Apple handles refunds. Request via reportaproblem.apple.com — usually approved if not too much time has passed.
