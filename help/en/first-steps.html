How to get started with Lupibu

2 min read

In short: Sign in with Apple, go through a short onboarding with your baby's profile — and Lupibu is ready to log sleep, feeds, and everything else. The more you log, the sharper the predictions get.

Lupibu works from the first minute. There's no long setup.

The first minutes

  • Sign in with Apple — it's the only sign-in method; there's no separate account creation.
  • Go through onboarding: your child's profile (name and birthday — the date is how we match predictions to age), your goals, and a few questions about your routine.
  • Onboarding ends with a subscription screen offering a 7-day free trial. To be honest with you: after the trial the subscription becomes paid. You can cancel anytime — see "How to cancel Pro".
  • Done — you're on the home screen.

What to do next

The home screen has tiles for quick logging: sleep, feeds, diaper, walk, and the rest. Tap any tile to start a timer or add an entry.

When predictions show up

Your first wake window and next-sleep prediction appear after you log one or two sleeps. In 3–5 days of regular use, accuracy climbs noticeably — the app is learning your baby's actual rhythm.

Don't aim for perfect records. A missed nap or an approximate time is fine. Lupibu is built to help, not to demand precision.
