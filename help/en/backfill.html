How to log sleep or events after the fact

2 min read

In short: Any entry can be added manually — yesterday, last week, whenever. Pick the type on the home screen and enter the time.

Forgot to start a timer? Didn't log night sleep? That's fine — Lupibu doesn't demand perfect real-time records.

Adding an entry after the fact

  • On the home screen, tap the tile for the entry type (e.g. "Sleep")
  • Enter start and end time manually
  • Save

Even faster with voice

Dictate to the AI assistant: "Last night he fell asleep at 8 and woke at 6" — it creates the entry with the right time.

Works offline

Sleep and breastfeeding timers can be stopped without a connection — the entry saves on device and syncs when you're back online.

Does this affect predictions?

Yes — predictions use all entries, whenever they were added. Backfilled entries work the same as real-time ones.
