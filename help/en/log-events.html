What and how to log during the day

2 min read

In short: Log the essentials: sleep, feeds, diapers. The rest is optional. More data means better predictions, but even the minimum is useful.

Lupibu supports 12 entry types, but you don't have to log all of them. Start with what matters for your child.

What's worth logging

  • Sleep — naps and night sleep. The basis for predictions.
  • Feeds — breast, bottle, solids. Affects the day's rhythm.
  • Diapers — especially in the first months (pediatricians often ask).

Optional, as needed

  • Walks, massage, bath — if you want to link them to sleep or mood.
  • Medical: medication, doctor visits, vaccination, symptoms.
  • "Other" for anything non-standard.

How this helps

All entries form your history and feed predictions and weekly reports. The AI assistant uses them to answer questions about your baby's routine.

Don't aim for completeness. Two or three entries a day is already useful. A missed day doesn't break stats.
