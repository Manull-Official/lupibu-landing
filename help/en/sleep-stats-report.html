How to view sleep stats and share a report

2 min read

In short: The Rhythm tab shows 7-day sleep cards and averages. "Download PDF report" generates a 10-day PDF and sends it through the iOS share sheet.

All sleep analytics live on the Rhythm tab in the bottom bar.

What it shows

  • Cards for the last 7 days — each day as a 24-hour horizontal bar with all sleeps.
  • Averages: wake and bedtime, awake window, total sleep, naps and night sleep.
  • Comparison with the norm — how your baby sleeps relative to the age range.

Feeds are not on this screen — they only appear in the PDF report.

Time range

The screen covers the last 7 days; the PDF report covers 10. There is no custom range picker.

Downloading the PDF report

  • Tap the "Download PDF report" button
  • A 10-day PDF is generated: child name, day-by-day timeline, averages vs the norm, and feeds
  • The iOS share sheet opens — send via mail, AirDrop, messenger
