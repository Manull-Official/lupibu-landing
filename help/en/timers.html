Sleep, feeding, and walk timers

2 min read

In short: Three entry types have timers: sleep, breastfeeding, walk. Tap the tile to start. Tap again to stop — entry saves automatically.

Timers exist so you don't have to remember exact times or fill a form after the fact.

Sleep timer

Tap "Sleep" on the home screen once your baby is down. When they wake, open the app and tap "Wake up". Optionally rate sleep quality.

If you forget to stop the timer — no drama. You can edit the end time manually.

Breastfeeding timer

Tap "Breast" and pick a side. The app remembers which side you used last time — easy to alternate. Tap again to stop and save.

Walk timer

Same as sleep: start when you leave, stop when you're back.

If a timer ran too long

Happens — forgot to stop. Open the entry in history and fix the end time. Predictions recalculate.
