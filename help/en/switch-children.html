Switching between children

1 min read

In short: Tap the name pill in the home header to pick a different child. The app remembers your choice across launches.

With more than one child, Lupibu gives you a fast switcher.

Where it is

At the top of the home screen — a pill with the child's name and photo (between the menu icon on the left and the AI icon on the right). Tap to open the list.

How to switch

  • The list shows all your children
  • Tap the one you want
  • Every screen — home, insights, history, AI chat — immediately shows that child's data

What's remembered

The active child persists between app launches. Switching does nothing to the other children's data.

With only one child

The pill is still visible — with the photo and name. Tapping it opens the same picker sheet, where you can add a second child directly (see "How to add another child").
