Switching between children

1 min read

In short: The name pill sits on the right side of the home header. Tap the pill to open the list; tap the peeking avatar next to it to cycle to the next child. Your choice persists across launches.

With more than one child, Lupibu gives you a fast switcher.

Where it is

On the right side of the home header — a pill with the active child's name and photo. With several children, the next child's avatar peeks out from behind the pill.

How to switch

  • Tap the peeking avatar — instantly cycles to the next child, round-robin
  • Tap the pill itself — opens the list of all children; tap the one you want
  • Every screen — home, rhythm, history, assistant chat — immediately shows that child's data

What's remembered

The active child persists between app launches. Switching does nothing to the other children's data.

With only one child

The pill is still visible — with the photo and name. Tapping it opens the same picker sheet, where you can add a second child directly (see "How to add another child").
