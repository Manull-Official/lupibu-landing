How to log an event by voice

2 min read

In short: Tap the mic on the Assistant tab and speak naturally: "fell asleep at 2:20", "doctor visit yesterday at 11", "fed for 10 minutes". The AI parses it and creates the entry right away.

Voice is the fastest way to log something — especially hands-free.

Where to find it

The mic button lives in the assistant chat — the Assistant tab in the bottom bar. There is no mic in the entry form; voice lives in the chat only.

What you can say

  • Live: "Fell asleep", "Woke up", "Changed diaper"
  • After the fact: "Last night he fell asleep at 8 and woke at 6"
  • With details: "Bottle, 4 ounces, at 9 am"
  • Medical: "Saw the pediatrician at 11, prescribed ibuprofen"

How recording works

  • One voice note is up to 15 seconds — after that, recording stops and goes to processing on its own.
  • If you go quiet, recording also stops automatically — nothing to press.

Languages

Works in English, Russian, Kazakh. The recognition language follows the app language.

If the AI misheard

There's no draft step — the entry is created immediately and you'll see a "Recorded" confirmation. Wrong time or details? Open the entry in history and fix any field.

You don't need robot commands — natural speech works better.
