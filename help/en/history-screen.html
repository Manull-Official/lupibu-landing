The History screen: filters, period, edit & delete

2 min read

In short: History opens via the "View history" button on home. Four period presets (24 hours, yesterday, 7 days, custom range — up to a month), a type filter; tapping an entry opens details with edit and delete.

Full entry history isn't a tab — it's a dedicated screen. Open via the "View history" button in the recent events block on home.

Period selection

Top of the screen — presets:

  • "24 hours" — quick preset, no setup
  • "Yesterday" — the whole previous day
  • "7 days" — the last week
  • Custom range — opens a calendar; pick two dates. One month maximum

Type filter

Next to it — a filter: "All" or a single type (sleep, feed, diaper, etc.). Applies instantly.

Grouping

Entries group by day with headers like "Today", "Yesterday", "Sat, Mar 29". Inside a day: newest at the top.

Edit or delete

  • Tap an entry to open a details view
  • It has the "Edit" and "Delete" buttons right there
  • Editing opens the same form used for creation — changes save immediately

Back-dating an entry

You can add something in the past from here or from home — see "Logging after the fact".
