The History screen: filters, period, edit & delete

2 min read

In short: History opens from the "Recent events" block on home. Two period modes (last 24h or custom), a type filter, and long-press to edit or delete.

Full entry history isn't a tab — it's a dedicated screen. Open via "See all" in the "Recent events" block on home.

Period selection

Top of the screen — a mode switch:

  • "Last 24 hours" — quick preset, no setup
  • "Custom range" — opens a calendar; pick two dates to define the range

Type filter

Next to it — a dropdown: "All types" or a single type (sleep, feed, diaper, etc.). Applies instantly.

Grouping

Entries group by day with headers like "Today", "Yesterday", "Sat, Mar 29". Inside a day: newest at the top.

Edit or delete

  • Tap an entry to see details
  • Long-press — context menu: "Edit" or "Delete"
  • Editing opens the same form used for creation — changes save immediately

Back-dating an entry

You can add something in the past from here or from home — see "Logging after the fact".
