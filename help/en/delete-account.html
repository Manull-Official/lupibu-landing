Deleting a child or your account

2 min read

In short: Delete a child: Profile → child card → Delete. Delete account: red button at the bottom of Profile → two confirmations, data is wiped immediately.

Lupibu doesn't hide deletion — it's two taps, no support tickets.

Delete a child

  • Open Profile → tap the child card
  • On the edit screen, at the bottom — "Delete child" (red)
  • Confirm

Delete account

  • Profile → scroll to the bottom → red "Delete account" button
  • A sheet appears with options (it may nudge you to manage the subscription first)
  • Second confirmation — "Really delete?" with a warning
  • After confirming: local data is cleared immediately (MMKV + AsyncStorage + RevenueCat), the account is deleted on the server

What's gone

  • All child profiles and their entries
  • AI chat history
  • The subscription itself — auto-renew has to be canceled separately via Apple ID if you're still in trial (see "How to cancel Pro")

Restore

Once the account is deleted, there is no restore — you'd start from scratch. So think twice, or write us first: contact@lupibu.com.
