How to add another child

1 min read

In short: Profile → "Add child". Up to 4 children per account. Each child has its own entries and predictions.

One account supports up to 4 children.

How to add

  • Open the Profile tab (menu in the header)
  • Scroll to the children list → tap "Add child"
  • Fill in name, birth date, gender; photo optional
  • Save

Limit

  • Up to 4 children per account

Each child has its own history

Wake windows, predictions, and stats are computed per profile. One child's entries never feed into another child's forecasts.

Switch between children via the name pill on the home screen — see "Switching between children".
