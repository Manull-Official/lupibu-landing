How to edit or delete an entry

1 min read

In short: Tap the entry in history to open edit. Delete via the trash icon with confirmation.

Wrong time, wrong type, wrong details — always fixable.

Edit

  • Open "History" or find the entry on home
  • Tap it — edit form opens
  • Change fields
  • Save

Delete

  • Open the entry
  • Tap the trash icon
  • Confirm

Recovery

Deleted entries can't be recovered. When in doubt, edit instead.

Does this affect predictions?

Yes — edits recalculate predictions and stats instantly.
