Exporting data and deleting your account

2 min read

In short: Sleep report exports as PDF from the Rhythm tab. A full CSV history export isn't in the app yet. Deleting the account is a 2-step flow in Profile.

Straight talk, no marketing.

What you can export today

  • 10-day sleep PDF report — Rhythm tab → "Download PDF report" button. Includes: day-by-day timeline, averages, age-range norms, feeds.
  • It goes through the iOS share sheet — mail, AirDrop, messenger.

What's not there yet

There is no "export everything as CSV/JSON" button inside the app. If you need a full dump for a GDPR request, email contact@lupibu.com — we'll prepare one manually in a reasonable time.

We'd rather not promise what isn't built. A proper CSV export is planned.

Delete the account

Profile → the red "Delete Account" button at the bottom → two confirmations. See "Delete account or child" for details.
