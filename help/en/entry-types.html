12 entry types: what goes where

2 min read

In short: Sleep, feeds (breast/bottle/solids), diaper, walk, doctor, medication, vaccination, symptoms, massage, "other".

All 12 types are on the home screen. The most common ones are up top.

Interval (with timer)

  • Sleep — naps and night
  • Breast — side selection with auto-alternation
  • Walk

Point (single event)

  • Bottle — with volume
  • Solids — with food description
  • Diaper — pee/poop/both
  • Doctor — visit note
  • Medication — name, dose
  • Vaccination — vaccine name
  • Symptoms — fever, runny nose, rash
  • Massage
  • Other — free text

If the type you need isn't here, use "Other" with a description.
