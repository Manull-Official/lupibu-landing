Lupibu says "time to sleep" but baby isn't tired

2 min read

In short: The prediction is a likely window, not a command. If your baby is clearly alert and happy — wait 15–20 minutes and watch for tired cues.

Lupibu shows predictions with a small lead so you have time to settle baby before they overtire. But the app can't see the room.

What to do

  • Check baby: yawning, eye rubbing, fussing?
  • If not — wait 15–20 minutes and check again
  • If "second wind" — sudden hyperactivity — they're already overtired. Settle fast

Why this is fine

Predictions use your average wake window. Day to day it varies by ±30–60 minutes. When today's window runs longer — log the sleep when it happens, predictions update.

What NOT to do

Don't force sleep by the timer — it leads to long settling and tears. Your baby's cues always win.
