The Profile & Settings screen

3 min read

In short: Everything account-related lives on one Profile screen (menu icon in the header): name, language, theme, notifications, analytics, kids, Pro, and account deletion.

Lupibu doesn't have a separate settings menu — it's all on one Profile screen. Open it via the menu icon on the left of the home header.

At the top

  • Display name — edit inline: tap the pencil, type, save
  • Sign-in method — Apple Sign In only. Email is not shown in the UI; only a Firebase ID is used internally

Children

  • List of your kids (up to 4 children)
  • "Add child" at the bottom of the list
  • Tap a card to edit

App settings

  • Language — English, Русский, Қазақша. Change syncs to cloud and applies instantly
  • Theme — light, dark, or follow the system
  • Notifications — toggle; if iOS permission is off, a hint + "Open Settings" shortcut appears
  • Analytics — toggle; turn it off and we stop collecting usage data right away. The app keeps working exactly the same

Support & legal

  • Help Center — this page
  • Feedback — in-app form; replies go to the same account
  • Legal — Privacy Policy, Terms, Medical Disclaimer

Danger zone at the bottom

  • Logout — gray button, asks to confirm
  • Delete account — red, two-step flow. See "Delete account or child"
