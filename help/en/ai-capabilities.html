What the AI assistant can and cannot do

3 min read

In short: The AI answers questions about your baby's routine using your actual data. It does NOT replace a pediatrician or make diagnoses.

The Lupibu AI isn't a generic chatbot. It sees your baby's data and responds with that context.

What the AI can do

  • Answer routine questions: "How much did he sleep today?", "When was the last feed?", "Is our wake window normal for 6 months?"
  • Create entries from voice or text — including backfilled events
  • Explain predictions — why a sleep is predicted now, where the wake window came from
  • Give morning tips — personalized daily hints based on your pattern
  • Talk through common scenarios — growth spurts, regressions, starting solids

What it does NOT do

  • No medical diagnoses
  • No medication or dosing advice — pediatrician only
  • Doesn't replace a doctor for worrying symptoms
  • Not 100% accurate — it's an assistant, not an authority

Good questions to ask

  • "Why did he wake so often last night?"
  • "Should I shorten naps if nights are rough?"
  • "Which wake window has been stable for us lately?"

When to call the pediatrician instead

Any physical symptoms, sudden behavior changes, developmental concerns. The AI can prepare a data summary to show your doctor — that's its most useful role in those cases.
